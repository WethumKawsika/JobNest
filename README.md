# UniWork
## 📱 Overview  
**UniWork** is a mobile app designed to help Sri Lankan university students easily find part-time job opportunities.  
Job owners can post their part-time job details, and students can search, filter, and apply for suitable jobs — all within one app.  

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement  
Many university students in Sri Lanka rely on part-time jobs to manage daily expenses, but finding suitable work is difficult.  
There is no single, convenient platform that connects job owners with students.  
**UniWork** bridges this gap — providing verified part-time job listings with clear details like salary, timing, and benefits.  

---

## 🚀 Key Features  

### 👥 For Job Owners  
- Post new part-time job listings  
- Include essential job details such as:  
  - 💰 **Salary range:** (e.g., Rs. 1000–2000/day)  
  - 🧰 **Work type:** Promotion, Tuition, Delivery, etc.  
  - 🍱 **Food availability:** Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, etc.  
  - 🚗 **Transport:** Provided or not  
  - ⏰ **Work time:** Morning / Evening / Night  
  - 📍 **Location:** City or area name  
  - 👨‍💼 **Required persons:** Male/Female, Count  
  - 🎂 **Age limit (optional)**  

### 🎓 For Students  
- Browse or search part-time jobs  
- Filter by salary, time, type, or location  
- Save favorite jobs for later  
- Contact job owners directly (via phone or chat)  
- Receive notifications for new job posts

## 🛠️ Tech Stack  

| Component | Technology |
|------------|-------------|
| **Language** | Kotlin |
| **Framework / IDE** | Android Studio |
| **Database** | Firebase Realtime Database / Firestore |
| **Authentication** | Firebase Auth (Email/Google Login) |
| **Location Services** | Google Maps API |

---

## 🧩 App Flow  

1. **Login / Register** – Students and job owners sign in via Firebase Auth  
2. **Job Posting** – Job owners add details and publish the job  
3. **Job Browsing** – Students browse and filter jobs by category  
4. **Application / Contact** – Students contact the job owner  
5. **Notification System** – New jobs trigger push notifications
